适用范围 / Scope

本政策适用于原 Hip Interlude 更新后的 HipLab 开髋训练 iOS 基础版，Bundle ID 为 com.ugk.hipinterlude。其他 Bundle ID 和 Android 版沿用其各自政策。

This policy covers the iOS base release of HipLab, formerly Hip Interlude, with bundle ID com.ugk.hipinterlude. Other bundle identifiers and the Android app have separate policies.

不收集个人数据 / No personal data collected

应用无需账号，不含广告、行为分析、第三方崩溃上报、支付或开发者云端同步。本版隐藏在线 AI 入口且不配置 AI Key，不向 AI 服务发送内容或创建新的 AI 安装标识。开发者不会通过本版自动收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据，也不进行跨应用追踪。

No account is required. This release has no ads, analytics, third-party crash reporting, payments or developer-operated cloud sync. Online AI is disabled, with no AI key, requests or new AI installation identifier. The developer does not automatically collect, upload, sell or share personal data through this release and does not track users across apps.

本机记录与素材 / On-device records and media

安全提示回答、练习身份、自测结果、疼痛或麻木反馈、训练日期与结果、自定义训练组、提醒安排和偏好保存在应用私有存储中，开发者不能远程读取。旧版本的本机记录可能在升级后保留。训练视频与语音随包提供，基础训练离线可用；播放器仅通过本机 127.0.0.1 媒体服务读取资源，不向外网传送训练记录。

Safety answers, practice profiles, self-checks, discomfort feedback, practice dates and results, custom routines, reminder schedules and preferences remain in private on-device storage. The developer cannot remotely read them. Existing local records may remain after an update. Videos and audio are bundled and core training works offline. The player reads media through a localhost service bound to 127.0.0.1; it does not upload practice records.

后台播放与权限 / Background playback and permissions

用户主动开始训练或语音自测后，后台音频用于动作提示、倒数和换边口令；结束或退出会话后停止并释放，语音与提示音均关闭时不启用。应用不录音，不读取耳机或蓝牙设备标识，不请求相机、麦克风、定位、联系人或 HealthKit 数据。iOS 的无线数据询问本身不表示应用向开发者上传个人数据。

Background audio supports user-started training and spoken self-check cues, countdowns and side changes. Playback stops and its session is released on completion or exit; it is not enabled when voice and chimes are both off. The app does not record audio, read headphone or Bluetooth identifiers, or request camera, microphone, location, contacts or HealthKit data. An iOS wireless-data prompt does not itself mean personal data is uploaded.

备份与删除 / Backup and deletion

系统可能依 iOS、iCloud 或电脑备份设置备份和恢复应用数据。备份由 Apple 或用户选用的备份服务管理，开发者不接收这些备份。在 iOS 中“删除 App”移除应用和本地数据；“卸载 App”可能保留数据。删除应用不保证删除既有备份，恢复时可能取回记录。请在对应系统备份设置中管理。

iOS, iCloud or computer backups may include and restore app data according to your settings. Apple or your chosen backup service handles these backups; the developer does not receive them. Delete App removes the app and local data, while Offload App may retain documents and data. Deleting the app does not erase existing backups, and restoring a backup may restore records. Manage backups in the relevant system settings.

外部服务与主动联系 / External services and support

主动访问公开网页、发送邮件或提交 GitHub Issues 时，相应网站、服务提供方及接收者会处理你主动提供的联系信息或内容。网页托管服务可能依其政策处理访问技术日志。这些不是应用自动收集的训练数据。请勿在公开 Issues 中发布健康、自测或身份信息。

If you visit public pages, email support or open a GitHub issue, the relevant website, service provider and recipient process the information you choose to provide. Website hosting providers may process technical access logs under their own policies. This is separate from automatic collection in the app. Do not post health records, self-checks or identity information in public issues.

成年人健身 / Adult general fitness

本应用面向成年人，不面向儿童；提供一般健身练习，不是医疗器械，不用于诊断、治疗、治愈或预防疾病。出现疼痛、麻木或不适时应停止并咨询合格专业人员。

HipLab is intended for adults, not children, and provides general fitness practice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease. Stop if you experience pain, numbness or discomfort and seek qualified professional advice.
