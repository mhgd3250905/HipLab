使用 HipLab / Using HipLab

完成首次安全提示并选择练习身份，从“今日”选择训练；“练习”提供动作、套路与自测，“进度”展示本机练习记录。界面目前主要为中文，语音支持中文与英语。无需注册，视频与语音内置。本版无在线 AI、广告、订阅或应用内购买。久坐提醒目前仅保存安排，尚不投递系统通知。

Complete the initial safety prompts and choose a practice profile, then start from Today. Practice contains exercises, routines and self-checks; Progress shows local records. The interface is primarily in Chinese and spoken cues support Chinese and English. No account is needed and media is bundled. This release has no online AI, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Reminder settings currently save a schedule without delivering system notifications.

语音与中断 / Audio and interruptions

检查应用的语音/提示音开关、媒体音量和当前音频输出。训练受电话或其他音频中断后，请回到训练页核对状态再继续。结束或退出训练会停止引导播放。请勿突然调高音量。

Check voice/chime settings, media volume and the active output device. After a call or another interruption, return to the training page and check the session before resuming. Finish or exit the session to stop guidance. Avoid sudden increases in volume.

记录与备份 / Records and backups

记录保存在本机，没有开发者账号同步；系统备份恢复由设备设置决定。删除前请阅读隐私政策。应用用于一般健身；疼痛、麻木或不适时请停止并咨询专业人员。

Records stay on your device; there is no developer account sync. Restore behavior depends on your system backup settings. Read the privacy policy before deletion. The app is for general fitness: stop and seek qualified advice if you experience pain, numbness or discomfort.
