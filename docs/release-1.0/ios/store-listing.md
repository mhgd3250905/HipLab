适用候选：`com.ugk.hipinterlude` / `1.0.0 (20)`，复用 Hip Interlude（Apple ID `6806806172`）。仅交付开哥审阅和手动替换；未保存到 App Store Connect。

## 简体中文

名称：HipLab 开髋训练

副标题：动作示范、语音引导与本地练习记录

推广文本：跟随内置动作视频和语音口令，安排自己的髋部活动与柔韧练习。无需账号，基础训练离线可用。

关键词：开髋,开胯,髋部,活动度,柔韧,拉伸,久坐,舞蹈,语音训练,训练记录

描述：

HipLab 是面向成年人的髋部活动与柔韧练习助手。选择适合自己的练习安排，跟随动作示范和语音口令，逐步完成动作、换边与休息。

- 今日训练：按自己的场景与时间安排练习。
- 动作与套路：浏览内置动作示范和要点，单独练习动作，或创建自己的训练组。
- 简单自测：根据示范和自己的感受记录髋部活动情况，作为日常练习参考。
- 语音引导：提供动作预告、倒数、换边和要点提示，支持中文与英语音频。
- 本地进度：在设备上查看练习日期、自测及训练记录，选择浅色或深色主题。
- 离线使用：视频与语音随应用提供，基础练习无需账号或联网。

界面目前以中文为主。本版不提供在线 AI，不含广告、订阅或应用内购买。久坐提醒设置仅保存时间安排，尚不投递系统提醒通知。

训练、自测、疼痛反馈和偏好保存在设备的应用私有存储中，开发者不会自动上传这些记录。系统备份与恢复由设备设置管理。

HipLab 提供一般健身练习，不是医疗器械，不用于诊断、治疗、治愈或预防疾病，不能替代专业医疗建议。如有疼痛、麻木、受伤或其他不适，请停止练习并咨询合格专业人员。

## English (US)

Name: HipLab: Hip Mobility

Subtitle: Guided movement and practice

Promotional text: Follow bundled exercise videos and spoken cues for your own hip mobility practice. No account required. Essential training works offline.

Keywords: hip,mobility,flexibility,stretch,desk,glute,dance,routine,voice,exercise

Description:

HipLab helps adults build a personal hip mobility and flexibility practice. Follow included exercise demonstrations and spoken cues, then review your practice records on your own device.

- Daily practice: choose a session for your setting and available time.
- Exercises and routines: browse demonstrations and tips, practice individual movements, and create your own training groups.
- Self-checks: record your own observations of hip movement. These are exercise references, not medical assessments or automated measurements.
- Voice guidance: hear exercise previews, countdowns, side changes and technique cues in Chinese or English.
- Local progress: review practice dates, self-checks and training records. Choose a light or dark theme.
- Offline essentials: videos and audio are included. Core practice needs no account or internet connection.

The interface is primarily in Chinese. This release has no online AI, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Sitting reminder settings save a schedule but do not deliver system notifications.

Practice records, self-checks, discomfort feedback and preferences stay in private on-device storage. The developer does not automatically upload these records. Device backups and restores follow your system settings.

HipLab is for general fitness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease. Stop exercising if you experience pain, numbness, injury or discomfort, and seek qualified professional advice.
