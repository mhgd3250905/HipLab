## App name

HipLab: Hip Mobility

## Short description

Offline hip mobility practice with exercise demos, voice cues and local records.

## Full description

HipLab helps adults build a personal hip mobility and flexibility practice. Follow bundled exercise demonstrations and spoken cues, then review your practice records on your own device.

• Daily practice: choose a session and follow its exercises, side changes and rest periods.
• Exercises and routines: browse movement demonstrations and tips, practice individual exercises, and create or edit your own training groups.
• Self-checks: record your own observations of hip movement using visual examples. These are personal exercise references, not medical assessments or automated measurements.
• Voice guidance: hear exercise previews, countdowns, side changes and technique cues. Adjust voice and cue volumes.
• Local progress: review practice dates, self-checks and training records. Choose a light or dark theme.
• Offline essentials: exercise videos and audio are included. Basic practice does not require an account or an internet connection.

The app interface is primarily in Chinese, with Chinese and English voice guidance options. This English store description does not indicate a fully localized English interface.

The existing AI customization entry remains visible, but this release has no configured online AI service. Requests entered there use local preset fallbacks without calling an online model; those presets are not presented as AI-generated plans. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Training records, self-check answers, discomfort feedback and preferences stay in the app's private storage. The developer does not automatically upload these records through this basic release. Android or your device manufacturer may back up and restore app data according to your system settings. Removing local app data does not necessarily delete existing system backups.

A foreground service supports the guided audio you start and displays a training notification. Background and lock-screen behavior depends on the device and operating system. Sitting reminder settings currently save a schedule only; this release does not deliver reminder notifications.

HipLab is for general fitness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Stop exercising if you experience pain, numbness, an injury or other discomfort.
